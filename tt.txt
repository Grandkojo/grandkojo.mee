    <h2>Django Custom Permissions</h2>
    <p>
    Wanted to ever create specific permissions to a group of users easily? This guide would help you get started. We would create a simple blog post application, where we'd have two users, an editor and a user, assign permissions to them, where the editor in this case, is the only one who can delete `published` post of users.
